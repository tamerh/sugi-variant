About Sugi Variant

A deterministic, cross-sourced reference page for every clinically significant human variant — open and free.

Sugi Variant assembles one reference page per clinically significant human variant. Anchored on ClinVar, each page integrates population frequency (gnomAD v4.1), computational predictors (AlphaMissense, REVEL, SaProt), evolutionary conservation (phyloP / GERP / phastCons), splice impact (SpliceAI), functional data (MaveDB), gene constraint and dosage, and — where a ClinGen expert panel has curated the variant — its applied ACMG criteria reproduced verbatim. Every fact is shown with its source and the dataset build date.

Deterministic, not generated. Nothing on a page is written by a language model. Each page is assembled by a fixed pipeline from primary databases, so it is reproducible and every number is traceable to its source and version.

We describe evidence; we do not compute a verdict. Sugi Variant reports what the authoritative sources say — ClinVar's classification, ClinGen's applied ACMG codes — and it never re-tallies or invents a classification of its own. Computational predictors are shown as independent agreement signals, not additive votes: AlphaMissense carries the ACMG weight for missense and conservation for non-missense, while REVEL, SaProt and others are shown as concurring or differing. This follows the ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation guidance that correlated predictors must not be counted as separate evidence.

The disagreement view. Because the evidence is laid out transparently rather than collapsed into one score, Sugi Variant can surface something no other resource does: the variants where the independent signals disagree — a predictor at odds with the clinical call, predictors split among themselves, or the predictors unanimously agreeing where ClinVar's submitters conflict. These are a quality-control signal for review, not a reclassification.

Sugi Variant is the per-variant companion to the deterministic Sugi Atlas, and resolves gene and protein identifiers through BioBTree.

Reference and research use only. Sugi Variant is not a diagnostic device and is not medical advice. Interpretation of a variant for an individual must be done by a qualified clinician or genetic counselor.

The site is free and open for everyone. For a custom build on your own data, get in touch.
