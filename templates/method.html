Method

How each variant page is assembled — deterministically, from primary data.

A full method and validation preprint is in preparation. This page is a short summary; it will be replaced by the complete manuscript when it is posted.

Scope. Sugi Variant builds a page for every ClinVar variant classified Pathogenic, Likely pathogenic, Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic, or Conflicting classifications of pathogenicity. The benign and uncertain long tail is excluded by design. Coverage spans protein-coding, non-coding RNA (n.) and mitochondrial (m.) genes.

Assembly. Pages are enumerated gene-first: for each gene, its qualifying ClinVar variations are collected, and each is annotated by joining primary datasets on genomic coordinate (gnomAD, AlphaMissense, REVEL, conservation, SpliceAI), on the UniProt protein change (SaProt), or by identifier (ClinGen VCEP curation, MaveDB, pathways). Every datum is retained with its source and the dataset's build date. The pipeline is fixed and deterministic — no model-generated text.

Evidence framing. Following ClinGen Sequence Variant Interpretation guidance, computational predictors are not treated as independent additive evidence: a single calibrated predictor carries the ACMG weight (AlphaMissense for missense; conservation for non-missense), and the others are shown as agreement signals. The "in-silico consensus" is a descriptive quality-control read, not a confidence score, and its main value is flagging discordant cases.

Authoritative assessments, verbatim. Where a ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panel has classified a variant, its applied and not-met ACMG criteria, curation rationale, specification version and Evidence Repository link are reproduced exactly as published. Sugi Variant reports these; it does not recompute or combine them.

Resolution. Variants are addressable by gene + HGVS (protein or coding), by dbSNP rsID, and by GRCh38 coordinate, via a precomputed index over the corpus.

Identifiers are resolved through BioBTree. Every fact on a variant page is traced to one of the sources below, with its dataset build date.
