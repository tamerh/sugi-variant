Structural variants

{{ total }} clinical structural variants — deletions, duplications, insertions and inversions from dbVar and ClinVar CNV submissions — with their overlapping genes and ClinVar accessions.

Structural variants are a separate corpus from the per-residue variant pages. Each page reports the source record (type, size, breakpoints, significance, origin, phenotypes), its ClinVar accessions and every gene whose Ensembl span overlaps the interval. The SNV predictors (AlphaMissense, REVEL, SaProt, SpliceAI) do not apply.

Every clinical CNV has one page: an entry linked to dbVar is served at its NSSV, and a ClinVar-only CNV at its VCV…. Open a variant at /sv/<NSSV…>, a dbVar region at /sv/<nsv…>, a ClinVar accession, or a locus such as /sv/9:72500000-72900000. Gene pages also list the structural variants overlapping the gene.
